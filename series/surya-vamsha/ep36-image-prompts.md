# E36 image prompts (Where Ganga comes from: from Vishnu's foot to Shiva's locks)

Each prompt below is only the **[SCENE]** part: the style prefix from [image-style.md](image-style.md) is added automatically. Recurring characters and places use their description lines word for word from [characters.md](characters.md): Vamana, Trivikrama (and his toe at the shell of the universe), King Bali, Lord Brahma, the seven sages among the stars (never counted: three clear, the rest fade), the river goddess Ganga (always with the god phrase; "her lower body made of falling white water" in the stream; a proud lifted chin only where the narration speaks of her pride), Ganga as a river of light, Lord Shiva (always with the god phrase), Shiva receiving Ganga (the line without the stream and with "eyes open, a calm half smile" before she falls into his locks, with the stream once she does), Bhagiratha in tapas (with "but now his eyes open" where he looks up, and his standing-on-one-leg add-on for the second tapas), Bhagiratha on his chariot with his two horses and the river close behind, the sage Jahnu (not yet named in the narration, so shown only as a sage), and the places Mount Kailash, the snowy tapas ledge, Bindusar and Jahnu's hermitage. One-off figures and places keep one fixed line each, repeated word for word: the universe `the universe as a vast golden egg-shaped shell floating alone in a boundless dark ocean of still water, the shell faintly glowing from within, nothing else outside it`; Dhruva `Dhruva, a radiant boy about ten, golden-brown skin, clean-shaven, no beard, no moustache, black hair in a small top-knot, plain white dhoti, a simple tulsi-bead necklace, exactly two arms, calm devoted face, no crown`; Mount Meru `Mount Meru, a colossal golden mountain rising far above the clouds, its broad flat summit crowned with a shining city of golden palaces, stars close above it`; Bali's sacrifice `the open sacrificial ground of the asura king, a square brick fire altar with a small steady fire, kusha grass mats, brass vessels, silk canopies on carved poles, no other fires` (shortened to `the sacrificial ground of the asura king, the altar fire well behind, no other fires` in picture 9, to keep the prompt in length); the Alakananda valley `a deep Himalayan valley, snowy peaks on both sides, a broad river of clear blue-green water rushing south over boulders between pine forests`; the lingam `a smooth black stone Shiva lingam on a round stone base in a small dim stone shrine, marigold flowers around it`; the devotee `a woman devotee in a simple ochre cotton saree, seen from the side, exactly two arms`; and the reflection figure from E33 `a lone small human figure in plain white cloth, exactly two arms, seen from behind`. The reflection lines (सूत्र-पंक्ति) are empty symbolic scenes with at most one small figure. Where only Vishnu's feet are seen (pictures 12, 14, 15, 27, 42) the body is out of frame, as characters.md allows for the toe at the shell. Shiva holds his trident in his left hand in every picture where it is seen. The thumbnail words "हिमालय से पहले" go in the calm dark space on the left of picture 3. No fire except the altar fire of Bali's sacrifice and the single clay lamps named in their prompts. Every prompt follows [picture-rules.md](picture-rules.md). Sources: Bhagavata 5.17 and 8.20-21, Valmiki Bala Kanda. 47 pictures, each shown from its **starts at** words until the next picture's words.
