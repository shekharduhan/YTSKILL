# E02 image prompts (Ikshvaku: the king born from a sneeze)

Style prefix for every prompt: see [image-style.md](image-style.md), including its "Lessons from
Episode 1". Each line below is the **[SCENE]** part. 34 images for 9.8 minutes (about 17 s each),
spread in proportion to each section's narration time. The scene column must use one label per script
section, in order (kept for reference).

The **starts at** column is the narration phrase at which the picture appears; assemble.py times each
picture to that phrase in the finished audio, so pictures never drift from the voice.

Recurring characters (same words every time):
- **Manu:** tall bearded king, greying hair, royal saffron robes, calm eyes.
- **Ikshvaku:** strong king, trimmed dark beard, golden crown, deep red robe with a sun emblem.
- **Vikukshi (Shashada):** young prince, long black hair, light armour, blue-green angavastram, bow.
- **Vasishtha:** elderly sage, long white hair and beard tied in a top-knot, saffron robes, staff.
- **Shri Ram:** blue-skinned, golden bow.
