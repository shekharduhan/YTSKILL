# E37 image prompts (Jahnu drinks the Ganga)

Each prompt below is only the **[SCENE]** part: the style prefix from [image-style.md](image-style.md) is added automatically. Recurring characters use their description lines word for word from [characters.md](characters.md): King Bhagiratha on his chariot (always followed by his two chariot horses; "but now standing still" where the chariot has halted, and "his head turned back over his shoulder" when he looks back), Ganga as a river of light (with "flowing close behind the chariot, fish, turtles and faint figures of gods and gandharvas in white and gold along its banks" whenever she follows the chariot; never a face in the water), the sage Jahnu (with his soaked add-on after the flood, his drinking add-on "both palms cupped together and raised in front of his face, the river shrinking toward his cupped hands", never water entering his mouth, and his releasing add-on; "but now his eyes clearly closed in prayer" or "but now his eyes calm" where he is at peace), the river goddess Ganga (once, as Jahnu's daughter, with the god phrase), Lord Shiva with his receiving line (hook), the devas, the sage Vishvamitra, the boys Rama and Lakshmana, Prince Anshuman and the sage Kapila (one humility picture, Kapila's Patala shortened to `a dark cavern far below the earth, a soft blue-white glow` to keep the prompt in length), and the places Jahnu's hermitage, Patala, the ash mound of Patala, the great chasm and the Ganga meeting the sea. One-off figures keep one fixed line each, repeated word for word: Bhagiratha off his chariot for the plea, `King Bhagiratha on foot, about thirty, golden-brown skin, short neat black beard, long black hair tied back, simple golden crown, white silk dhoti and deep saffron angavastram, exactly two arms, no bow`; the gandharvas and sages who come with the devas, `gandharvas in pale blue silk and grey-bearded sages in ochre, fading into light`; the reflection figure from E33, `a lone small human figure in plain white cloth, exactly two arms, seen from behind`; the Sultanganj bend (Uttarvahini Ganga), `a broad bend of the Ganga on a wide green plain, the river turning to flow north, a great dark rocky outcrop rising from the water with a small ancient stone temple with a spire on top, sandy banks, palm trees, no modern buildings, no electric lights, no motor boats`; and the kanwar pilgrims, `pilgrims in plain saffron cotton dhotis, each carrying a bamboo kanwar pole on one shoulder with two small covered clay water-pots hanging from its ends, walking barefoot on a dusty earth track, the far figures fading into dust beyond counting`. The reflection lines (सूत्र-पंक्ति) are empty symbolic scenes with at most one small figure. The script's dry-riverbed screen asks for gasping fish: they are left out (rule 10), the bed is shown as wet stones and sand. The lineage card (Bhagavata 9.15) is drawn as a glowing lineage tree under the moon, with no names (rule 6). The thumbnail words "निगल ली" go in the calm empty dusk sky on the left of picture 3. No fire except the small altar fire of picture 11 and the clay lamps named in their prompts. Every prompt follows [picture-rules.md](picture-rules.md). The sources of this episode are Valmiki (Bala Kanda), the Bhagavata (9.15) and local tradition (Sultanganj). 46 pictures, each shown from its **starts at** words until the next picture's words.
