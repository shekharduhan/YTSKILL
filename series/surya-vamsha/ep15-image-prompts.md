# E15 image prompts (Trishanku: the two verses)

Each prompt below is only the **[SCENE]** part: the style prefix from [image-style.md](image-style.md) is added automatically. Recurring characters use their description lines word for word from [characters.md](characters.md); Shukadeva and Parikshit appear only here and keep one fixed line. Every prompt follows [picture-rules.md](picture-rules.md). Trishanku in the sky is always the cursed (chandala) form, because the Bhagavata says he was cursed before he rose. 39 pictures, each shown from its **starts at** words until the next picture's words.
