# E35 image prompts (Ganga's two objections: who will hold her, and who will wash her)

Each prompt below is only the **[SCENE]** part: the style prefix from [image-style.md](image-style.md) is added automatically. Recurring characters use their description lines word for word from [characters.md](characters.md): Bhagiratha in tapas (with "but now his eyes open" added wherever his face is seen turned to Ganga or Kailash, his hands always given a job, and his standing-on-one-leg add-on for the tapas to Shiva), young King Bhagiratha (one picture, arriving at the ledge, crown still on as in E34), the river goddess Ganga (always with the god phrase; "her lower body made of falling white water" when she descends; she never appears on earth as a river, only in visions that the narration marks as her own words), Ganga as a river of light, Lord Shiva (always with the god phrase; meditating "seated on a tiger skin on a snowy ledge, eyes clearly closed"; the receiving line with the stream only where the narration names Ganga in his locks; before the descent, the receiving line without the stream and "eyes open, a calm half smile"), Lord Vishnu (once, faintly in the sky, for "भगवान हरि"), and the places the snowy tapas ledge (shortened to `a high snowy ledge in the Himalaya` where two long character lines share a picture, to keep the prompt in length), Mount Kailash and the ash mound of Patala. One-off figures keep one fixed line each, repeated word for word: the saint `a saint, an elderly ascetic about seventy, thin, dark-brown skin, long white beard, long white hair in a top-knot, plain ochre cloth wrap over his left shoulder, rudraksha beads, peaceful face, exactly two arms, no staff, no crown`; the bathers `a crowd of ordinary bathers, men in plain white cotton dhotis, women in simple cotton sarees of muted ochre, green and blue, standing waist-deep in the river, the far figures fading into mist beyond counting`; the imagined river bank `a wide river bank, simple worn stone steps going down into a broad shining river, sandy shore, tall kusha grass, no buildings, no boats`; the sins as `faint grey smoke-like shadows without faces`; and the reflection figure from E33 `a lone small human figure in plain white cloth, exactly two arms, seen from behind`. The reflection lines (सूत्र-पंक्ति) are empty symbolic scenes with at most one small figure; the script's modern "भगीरथ प्रयत्न" screen (dam, bridge, laboratory) is drawn as timeless hand labour and three generations on a mountain path, because rule 7 forbids modern things. The thumbnail words "कौन थामेगा?" go in the empty sky on the left of picture 16. No fire except the single clay lamps named in their prompts. Every prompt follows [picture-rules.md](picture-rules.md). The source of this episode is the Bhagavata (9.9). 46 pictures, each shown from its **starts at** words until the next picture's words.
