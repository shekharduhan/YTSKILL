# E01 image prompts (Manu, the fish and the flood)

Style prefix for every prompt: see [image-style.md](image-style.md). Each line below is the
**[SCENE]** part. About one image per 15-20 seconds of narration: 30 images for 9.5 minutes; the
video holds each one with a slow zoom or pan.

The **starts at** column is the narration phrase at which the picture appears; assemble.py times each
picture to that phrase in the finished audio, so pictures never drift from the voice.

Recurring characters, described the same way in every episode:
- **Manu / Satyavrata:** tall bearded king, greying hair, white then royal saffron robes, calm eyes.
- **Vasishtha:** elderly sage, long white hair and beard tied in a top-knot, saffron robes, staff.
- **The fish (Matsya):** giant golden fish with a single horn, scales glowing, divine light.
