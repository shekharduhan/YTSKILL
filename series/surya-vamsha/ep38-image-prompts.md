# E38 image prompts (The day the sixty thousand ancestors were freed)

Each prompt below is only the **[SCENE]** part: the style prefix from [image-style.md](image-style.md) is added automatically. Recurring characters and places use their description lines word for word from [characters.md](characters.md): King Bhagiratha on his chariot (always followed by his two chariot horses; "but now standing still" where the chariot has halted), King Bhagiratha in old age (the return to Ayodhya), Bhagiratha in tapas, Ganga as a river of light (with "flowing close behind the chariot, fish, turtles and faint figures of gods and gandharvas in white and gold along its banks" whenever she follows the chariot, and "the shining stream reaching the edge of the ash mound, sparks of light rising from the ash" in Patala; never a face in the water), the river goddess Ganga (once, with the god phrase), Lord Brahma (with the god phrase; his front right hand holds the rosary, so it is that hand that blesses or points), King Sagara in old age, King Anshuman and King Dilipa son of Anshuman (in the vision, faint among golden clouds), Anshuman and Dilipa as old ascetics (one memory picture), King Rituparna, the charioteer Bahuka, the devas, and the places Patala, the ash mound of Patala, the great chasm, the Ganga meeting the sea, the snowy tapas ledge, the Ayodhya terrace, gates and court, and the gate of heaven. Kapila is not shown: only his empty seat in the blue-white glow, as characters.md asks. The sixty thousand princes appear only as ash and as countless points of light, never counted, never as bodies or faces. One-off figures and places keep one fixed line each, repeated word for word: Bhagiratha off his chariot (as in E37), `King Bhagiratha on foot, about thirty, golden-brown skin, short neat black beard, long black hair tied back, simple golden crown, white silk dhoti and deep saffron angavastram, exactly two arms, no bow`; and Haridwar, `the ghats of Haridwar at dawn, wide old stone steps down to the swift green Ganga, small stone temples with spires along the bank, clay oil lamps, no modern buildings, no electric lights, no clock tower, no bridges, no signboards`. Present-day scenes add `no modern clothes, no plastic, no phones`. The reflection lines (सूत्र-पंक्ति) are empty symbolic scenes with at most one small figure. The script's text screens (the Bhagavata label, भगीरथ → भागीरथी, the three names) are drawn without any writing (rule 6): the three names become a three-panel picture (Vishnu's foot, Jahnu's hermitage, Bhagiratha's chariot). No fire except the clay lamps named in their prompts and the court's lamps. Every prompt follows [picture-rules.md](picture-rules.md). The sources of this episode are the Bhagavata (9.9), Valmiki (Bala Kanda), geography and living tradition. 46 pictures, each shown from its **starts at** words until the next picture's words.
