# Image style (all episodes)

Matches the reference the user supplied: painted Indian-mythology illustrations, cinematic and warm.

## The look

- **Medium:** detailed digital painting with clean ink outlines, like a premium illustrated
  mythology comic. Not photoreal, not 3D, not anime.
- **Light:** golden hour and firelight; warm amber, saffron and deep red, against dusk purples and
  blues. Strong rim light on the figures, soft god-rays in interiors.
- **Places:** carved temple and palace architecture with domes, arches, pillars, red and gold
  drapery, oil lamps and torches; or wide landscapes under a dramatic sky.
- **People:** traditional Indian dress and jewellery (dhoti, angavastram, saree, crowns, beads).
  Rama is blue-skinned with a golden bow; sages have long white hair and beards, in saffron and
  ochre robes; kings wear jewelled crowns and red robes.
- **Framing:** one clear subject per image, full or three-quarter figure, eye-level or slightly
  low for heroes; symmetrical palace interiors for court scenes.
- **No text** in the images, no logos, no watermarks.

## Prompt template

> Polished digital painting in a premium animated Indian mythology film style, smooth cel-shaded
> colour with clean outlines, highly detailed, saturated warm golden-hour and firelight, amber,
> saffron and deep red against a dusk purple-blue sky, cinematic rim lighting, no text, no
> watermark. **[SCENE]**. Wide 16:9 landscape composition.

Keep the same wording at the start of every prompt so the whole series looks consistent. Describe
recurring characters the same way every time (see each episode's prompt list).

## Aspect ratio

The reference images are vertical (9:16, phone/Shorts). The 10-minute episodes are YouTube
long-form, so render **16:9** for the episodes and 9:16 only for Shorts cut from them.

## Lessons from Episode 1 (apply to every episode from E02 on)

- **Divine beings must look divine, never comic.** Image models default to cartoon faces (goldfish eyes,
  droopy whiskers, open mouths). Describe a calm, dignified face and add "sacred and majestic, not
  cartoonish, not comic".
- **Matsya (the fish):** "a colossal sleek streamlined golden fish like a giant mahseer, jewel-like scales,
  dignified serene face with a calm half-closed eye, no whiskers, no open mouth, a single short pointed
  golden spike on its brow like a small temple finial". The word "horn" alone gives a long unicorn spike.
- **Put the people the narration names in the picture.** The boat carries the king and the seven sages;
  an empty boat loses the story.
- **Show scale.** A god-sized being must be far larger than the humans beside it.
- **Follow the narration order.** The first image matches the first spoken line (E01: the drowned earth
  first, the fish only when the fish is mentioned).
- **Make 2-3 versions of the opening image and the thumbnail image** (about $0.04 each) and pick the best:
  the first seconds decide whether a viewer stays.
- The model often draws the rope to the mouth instead of the horn and adds letterbox bars or a
  signature scribble; check every image before assembling.
