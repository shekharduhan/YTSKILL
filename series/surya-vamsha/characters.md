# Character sheet: Surya Vamsha, Episodes 3-20

Rule 15 in `picture-rules.md`: every time a character below appears in a picture prompt, paste his or
her **description line word for word**, every time, in every prompt. Do not shorten it ("Vasishtha, the
old sage") and do not paraphrase it. If two people are in one picture, paste both lines and give each a
position ("on the left ..., on the right ..."). Then add what each hand does (rule 3) and finish the
prompt with "no text, no letters, no writing, no signature".

Lines are in English, 20-45 words. Each ends with what the character must NOT have, because the model
adds those things otherwise.

## 2. Sages (Vasishtha and Vishvamitra must never be confusable)

How to tell them apart at a glance:

| | Vasishtha | Vishvamitra |
|---|---|---|
| Hair and beard | pure white, long, neat | iron-grey with black streaks, thick, rough |
| Robe | bright saffron cloth | dark brown bark-cloth (valkala) + black deerskin over the left shoulder |
| Hand item | plain wooden staff | brass water-pot (kamandalu), no staff |
| Face | calm, gentle | fierce, frowning, burning eyes |
| Build | slender, slightly stooped | tall, broad-shouldered ex-warrior |

When both are in one picture, put Vasishtha on the LEFT and Vishvamitra on the RIGHT in every episode.

### Vishvamitra (विश्वामित्र), the king-turned-sage
