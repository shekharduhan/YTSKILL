# E24 image prompts (the second Harishchandra, part 1: the king who gave away his kingdom)

Each prompt below is only the **[SCENE]** part: the style prefix from [image-style.md](image-style.md) is added automatically. The source of this episode is the Markandeya Purana. Recurring characters and places use their description lines word for word from [characters.md](characters.md): Harishchandra uses the E20 line (crowned) in the opening and forest scenes until the gift, and his E24 line (no crown, undyed cotton) from the gift on, so the hook picture already uses the E24 line with the crown lying at the sage's feet (only one crown in any picture); the cremation-ground line appears only in the last picture, the teaser for E25. Shaibya uses her hardship line throughout, since she appears only from the leaving of Ayodhya; Rohitashva, the old brahmin of Kashi and the chandala master use their own lines. Vishvamitra always holds the brass water-pot in his left hand, so his right hand has the only gesture; he has no staff, so the blow on the queen is shown only as his raised hand and her stumbling. Harishchandra's line says "no bow": in the hunt he carries `exactly one hunting spear in his right hand, no other weapon`. The power of obstruction (विघ्न) uses the Kali shadow line from characters.md, as it asks. Two one-off lines, repeated word for word: the Vidyas are `the Vidyas, three graceful women made entirely of soft white-gold light, half transparent, long flowing hair of light, plain flowing garments of light, sorrowful faces with glistening tears, exactly two arms each, no jewellery, no crowns`, and the forest is `a dense green forest at golden hour, tall sal trees, hanging creepers, shafts of sunlight, thick undergrowth, no buildings`. Where a picture holds several full character lines, the place is given in a short form (for example `the stone ghats of ancient Kashi beside the Ganga, no modern buildings`) so the prompt stays within length. The month passing is shown with a thin crescent moon, never a calendar. Every prompt follows [picture-rules.md](picture-rules.md). 48 pictures, each shown from its **starts at** words until the next picture's words.
